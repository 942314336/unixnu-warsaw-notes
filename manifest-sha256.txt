binaries that stay on the build host, so the hash is the only public handle on them.

Notes:

- `kernel.macho` is not byte-reproducible at our pin, so its hash identifies the exact binary that
  went into that round's boot image, recorded before the board was fired; it cannot be re-derived
  from source.
- `boot-m5c-diag250.img` carries the `kernel.macho` of round 247 unchanged, because round 250 only
  swapped the Data seed.
- Both boot images were hashed on the build host and again on the firing host after `scp`, and the
  two values are equal character for character.
- `data-seed250-gpt70.img` is also the value recorded as `data_ramdisk_sha256` inside
  `payload.json` for rounds 250 and 251.
- `526415af...` is abbreviated on purpose: only the short form is in the round log, so a full hash
  would be an unverified claim.
